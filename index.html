The band

❦

David Miller ✝ Jarrod Feintuch

Lilac Triggers started with a simple complaint: the records we wanted to hear weren’t being made anymore. So we started making them.

Cinematic soundscapes, pulsating drums, lush synth pads, glassy guitars, driving bass lines, and dramatic vocals.
