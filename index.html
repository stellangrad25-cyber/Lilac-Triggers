theBand

Lilac Triggers is David Miller and Jarrod Feintuch.

We make atmospheric pop. Synth pads, lush guitars, bass and drums that keep pushing, and vocals that go big when the song calls for it.

David played in Future Unlimited before this and brings a really tasteful ear to everything we record.

Slow Blink is our first album. It's eleven songs, and it's out soon.

Influences: Drab Majesty · Boy Harsher · Tears for Fears · The Church · Icehouse · The Sound · New Order · Thompson Twins · The Smiths · Ariel Pink · The Cars · Duran Duran · Genesis · Ice Choir · Men I Trust · Roxy Music · Tamaryn
