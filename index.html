Naples, Florida

The band

❦

David Miller

David was born and raised in Naples, Florida, and has spent the last 20 years writing and producing songs for his own projects and other notable artists. When he’s not working on Lilac Triggers, he’s behind the kit for other local musicians.

He’s also a die-hard fan of 80s new wave and post-punk.

Jarrod Feintuch

Jarrod has been playing and performing in bands since 2001. Like David, the part he loves most about making music is the writing element.

He moved down from Baltimore, and it didn’t take long for the two of them to realize they were chasing the same sound.

❦

Lilac Triggers started with a simple complaint: the records we wanted to hear weren’t being made anymore. So we started making them.

Cinematic soundscapes, pulsating drums, lush synth pads, glassy guitars, driving bass lines, and dramatic vocals.
