theBand

Lilac Triggers make music for the hour after the party, when the lights are still on and nobody wants to go home.

Built on drum machines, chorus-drenched guitar and bass lines that lead from the front, the band pulls from the new wave and post-punk records that soundtracked rainy bus windows and bedroom walls. The songs are dark, but you can still dance to them.

Slow Blink is the first full-length record: ten songs about the moment you close your eyes and something changes.

For fans of: The Cure · Cocteau Twins · Depeche Mode · Molchat Doma · Boy Harsher
