theBand

Lilac Triggers make music for the hour after the party, when the lights are still on and nobody wants to go home.

The band is David Miller and Jarrod Feintuch, and together they make atmospheric pop. Smooth synth pads drift under lush, shimmering guitars while the bass and drums keep everything moving forward, and the vocals go for the big, dramatic moment every time. The songs are dark, but you can still dance to them.

David, formerly of Future Unlimited, brings an extremely tasteful ear to vocals, drums and production. Jarrod plays guitar and co-writes the songs.

Slow Blink is their first full-length record: eleven songs about the moment you close your eyes and something changes.

Influences: Drab Majesty · Boy Harsher · Tears for Fears · The Church · Icehouse · The Sound · New Order · Thompson Twins · The Smiths
